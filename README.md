# Adidas-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard


## Overview

The Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product contribution, and retailer performance across the United States.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive KPI cards, charts, geographic analysis, and filters. It enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify sales trends, compare regions, and support data-driven strategic planning.

## Business Requirements

The business requires an interactive dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

* Monitor total sales, operating profit, units sold, average selling price, and profit margin.
* Analyze monthly sales trends to identify seasonal patterns.
* Compare sales performance across regions and states.
* Evaluate the contribution of different retailers and product categories.
* Analyze sales performance across In-store, Online, and Outlet channels.
* Filter data by region, invoice date, year, month, and sales method.
* Identify top-performing products and retailers.
* Support strategic planning through interactive business reporting.

## Dataset

**Source:** Adidas US Sales Dataset (Excel)

The dataset contains Adidas sales records across the United States for 2020 and 2021.

The data includes information about retailers, invoice dates, regions, states, cities, products, prices, units sold, operating profit, operating margin, and sales methods.

The original dataset is not included in this repository. The dashboard and project documentation are provided to demonstrate the analysis and reporting workflow.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

| KPI                   | Description                                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Total Sales           | Total revenue generated from product sales.  |
| Total Profit          | Total operating profit generated from sales. |
| Total Units Sold      | Total number of products sold.               |
| Average Selling Price | Average price per unit sold.                 |
| Profit Margin         | Operating profit as a percentage of sales.   |

These KPIs provide stakeholders with a quick overview of business performance.

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. KPI Cards

Displays Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Units Sold, Average Selling Price, and Profit Margin.

**Purpose:** Provide a quick summary of overall business performance.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend

An area chart showing monthly sales performance from January to December.

**Purpose:** Identify seasonal patterns, high-performing months, and changes in sales performance.

### 3. State-wise Sales

A map visualization showing sales distribution across US states.

**Purpose:** Analyze geographic sales performance and identify high-performing locations.

### 4. Region-wise Sales

A donut chart representing the percentage contribution of different regions to total sales.

**Purpose:** Compare regional performance and understand each region's contribution to overall revenue.

### 5. Product-wise Sales

A horizontal bar chart displaying sales generated by different Adidas product categories.

**Purpose:** Identify top-performing products and evaluate product-level revenue contribution.

### 6. Retailer-wise Sales

A horizontal bar chart comparing sales performance across retailers.

**Purpose:** Identify leading retailers and evaluate their contribution to total sales.

### 7. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

* Year
* Month
* Region
* Invoice Date
* Sales Method

The Clear All Slicers button allows users to reset the applied filters and return to the overall dashboard view.
